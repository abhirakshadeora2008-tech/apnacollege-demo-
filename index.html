The 15-minute field check

A sharper look now saves a hard decision later.

Use our simple rhythm every time you walk a rye field. Notice, record, respond.

Request the field checklist ↗
01

Walk the pattern

Enter from three points and check low spots, headlands, and the crop’s center.

02

Check the leaves

Look under the canopy for lesions, discoloration, insects, and unusual stress.

03

Choose your response

Record what you find, compare it to your threshold, and act with intention.

A season-long view

Good protection is a rhythm, not a rescue.

  • Autumn: Start clean with seed and soil checks.
  • Spring: Scout early as growth accelerates.
  • Heading: Protect the crop’s most valuable window.
  • Harvest: Review the season and plan the next stand.
